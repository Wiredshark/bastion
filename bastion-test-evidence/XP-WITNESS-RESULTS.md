## ★ THE PLANT MADE THE DEFECT VISIBLE IN A SINGLE LINE

Under the plant the emit reads `xp=8.0 wood=5`. The tally is **present and correct** right
beside an award that ignored it — so the log shows *the scale was available and not
applied*, not merely that a number was small. That is the difference between a witness
that reports a value and one that reports a **relationship**.

## ★ THE TWO WITNESSES ARE INDEPENDENT — proven, not assumed

The plant moved XP from 120 → 24 **while the yield stayed at 15**. Had the yield moved
too, the two instruments would have been reading a shared accumulator and neither would
mean what it claims. Cross-checking them in the *same run* is what establishes that, and
it cost nothing extra.

## ⚠ THE GATHER SITE WAS THE WORST KIND OF GAP

`"bastion: gathered"` already existed and **looked complete** — job id, position, a clean
past-tense verb. It carried **no XP amount**. A reader would have had no reason to suspect
anything was missing; the line reads like a full account of the event. An absent field
inside a present line is harder to notice than an absent line, which is exactly why F8-C3
went unclosed this long.

## ⚠ AND A RULE THAT WOULD HAVE BEEN WRONG HERE

The yield row **refused** `fell.wood_count` as the drop amount — frozen at placement, so a
witness carrying it would report the prediction rather than the yield.

**For XP that same tally is the correct source.** XP is granted once, at the base cut, for
the whole tree; there is no per-cell XP event to read back from. Registered in the prereg
*before* the build, precisely so the yield row's rule would not be cargo-culted into a
place its premise does not hold. **Same value, opposite verdicts, because the quantity
differs.**
