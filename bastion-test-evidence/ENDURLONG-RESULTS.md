## ★ The decisive evidence is a MECHANISM, not a rate

L1 held `blocked_materials = 30` from tick **31,318 to 511,329** — **480,000
consecutive ticks** — with `food_stock = 0` throughout and a final census
identical to the 271k collapsed runs (`refused=304, materials=240`).

**A hazard model requires repeated independent chances to escape. The deadlock
provides none:** once the sow job occupies the cell whose seeds lie beneath it,
no path in the loop can break the cycle. That is why doubling the time bought
literally nothing — 8 maturations at 570k, the same as 8 at 271k.

## ★★ The complete picture, reconciling everything including what I withdrew

1. A colony **either** establishes the haul loop early **or** deadlocks.
2. Once deadlocked, it is **absorbing** — 480,000 ticks of proof.
3. Thriving colonies never deadlock; their yield is linear in run length.
4. **Mid-range outcomes come from deadlocking LATE, not escaping late.** The
   50-maturation run harvested 50 times and *then* jammed.

withdrawn** — a run landed at exactly 50 and it is real. What replaces it is

**I registered the wrong dichotomy.** The choice was never "binary vs
